# Statement reliability

Generated by `statement_checks.py` — 300 tickers sampled from 2,974 (seed 0).

## Summary

33 of 39 identity checks hold to within 0.1% on at least 99% of filings. Every failure is in the **cash flow statement**; not one is in the income statement or the balance sheet, both of which are internally consistent to the float32 noise floor on essentially every filing.

- **Cash flow / ARY** — operating + investing + financing + FX = net change in cash: holds for 86.7% of filings, median deviation 2.4e-08, p95 4.8%
- **Cash flow / ART** — operating + investing + financing + FX = net change in cash: holds for 89.4% of filings, median deviation 2.2e-08, p95 2.9%
- **Cash flow / ARQ** — operating + investing + financing + FX = net change in cash: holds for 91.2% of filings, median deviation 0.0e+00, p95 2.7%
- **Cash flow / ARQ** — operating cash flow − |capex| = free cash flow: holds for 97.5% of filings, median deviation 0.0e+00, p95 0.0%
- **Cash flow / ART** — operating cash flow − |capex| = free cash flow: holds for 97.9% of filings, median deviation 0.0e+00, p95 0.0%
- **Cash flow / ARY** — operating cash flow − |capex| = free cash flow: holds for 98.5% of filings, median deviation 0.0e+00, p95 0.0%

So: the income statement and balance sheet can be read as filed. The cash flow statement's *section subtotals* are sound, but roughly one filing in ten does not foot from the sections to the reported net change in cash, and that is the vendor's arithmetic rather than anything this repository does to it. The statements page prints the discrepancy as an explicit **unreconciled** line rather than distributing it silently across the sections.

Coverage is a separate matter and much lower — see below. The vendor publishes only three cash flow subtotals plus capex, D&A and SBC, so most of the operating section is a residual no matter how it is presented.

Regenerate with `python3 statement_checks.py --write-report`.

Deviations are relative to a denominator chosen per statement: revenue for the income statement, total assets for the balance sheet, and the largest of the three section subtotals for the cash flow statement. The `≤1e-6` column is the float32 noise floor — figures are stored as float32, which is about seven significant digits, so on a large balance sheet exact equality is not attainable even when the vendor's arithmetic is exact.
